== Risks & Watch Items

== Risks on the Radar

[%step]
* SMB churn ticked up after the March price change
* Two enterprise renewals slip into next quarter
* Support headcount hasn't kept pace with seat growth

[WARNING]
If SMB churn doesn't stabilize by next quarter, it starts to weigh on blended NRR.

== Roadmap

[.timeline]
* Q4 — SSO for all mid-market plans, not just Enterprise
* Q4 — Usage-based add-on pricing pilot with 12 design partners
* Q1 — Regional data residency, EU first, then APAC
* Q1 — Self-serve upgrade flow, cutting sales-assisted deals by ~20%

== Methodology

[%collapsible]
.How these numbers are calculated
====
ARR is annualized from committed contract value at quarter close, excluding one-time services revenue.
Net revenue retention includes expansion, contraction, and churn from the cohort of customers active exactly one year prior.footnote:[Finance methodology doc, rev. 14 — ask #finance-questions for the latest version.]
====
